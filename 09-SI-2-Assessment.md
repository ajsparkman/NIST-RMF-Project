# SI-2 - Flaw Remediation Assessment

## Control

SI-2 - Flaw Remediation

## Purpose

The purpose of this control is to ensure that known software and system flaws are identified, corrected, and tracked in a timely manner.

## Assessment Question

Does Sparkman Systems LLC have an effective process for applying patches and correcting known security flaws?

## Evidence Requested

To assess SI-2, the following evidence would be requested:

- Patch management policy or procedure
- Windows Update configuration
- Recent patch compliance reports
- Records of critical security updates
- Remediation timelines for high-risk vulnerabilities
- Evidence showing failed or overdue patches are tracked

## Evidence Reviewed

The following fictional evidence was reviewed:

- Windows Update was enabled on company laptops and servers
- Critical Microsoft security updates were generally installed automatically
- No formal patching deadline or service-level target was documented
- Three systems had critical security updates more than 30 days overdue
- Failed patch installations were handled manually by the IT administrator
- No centralized patch compliance report was maintained

## Assessment Result

Status: Partially Satisfied

## Finding

Sparkman Systems LLC applies security updates through Windows Update, but it does not have a formal patch management timeline or centralized method for tracking patch compliance. Several systems had critical updates more than 30 days overdue.

## Risk

Delayed security updates may leave known vulnerabilities exposed for longer than necessary, increasing the risk of exploitation.

## Recommendation

Establish formal patching timelines based on vulnerability severity, track patch compliance centrally, and document exceptions or failed installations until remediation is complete.
