# AC-2 - Account Management Assessment

## Control

AC-2 - Account Management

## Purpose

The purpose of this control is to ensure that user accounts are properly created, managed, reviewed, disabled, and removed when they are no longer needed.

## Assessment Question

Does Sparkman Systems LLC have an effective process for managing user accounts throughout their lifecycle?

## Evidence Requested

To assess AC-2, the following evidence would be requested:

- Current Active Directory user account list
- Employee onboarding procedure
- Employee termination/offboarding procedure
- Account review records
- List of disabled and inactive accounts

## Evidence Reviewed

The following fictional evidence was reviewed:

- Active Directory contained 28 enabled user accounts
- Sparkman Systems LLC currently has 25 employees
- Two enabled accounts belonged to former employees
- One generic shared account was still active
- The company had a documented onboarding process
- The company did not have a documented quarterly account review process

## Assessment Result

Status: Partially Satisfied

## Finding

Sparkman Systems LLC does not consistently disable or remove accounts that are no longer required. Two former employee accounts remained enabled, and no formal recurring account review process was documented.

## Risk

Inactive or unnecessary accounts could be used by unauthorized individuals to gain access to company systems and data.

## Recommendation

Implement a formal account review process and require terminated employee accounts to be disabled promptly as part of the offboarding procedure.
