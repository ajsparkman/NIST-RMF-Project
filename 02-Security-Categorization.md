# Sparkman Systems LLC - Security Categorization

Sparkman Systems LLC will be evaluated based on the potential impact of a loss of confidentiality, integrity, or availability.

## Confidentiality

Impact Level: Moderate

Unauthorized disclosure of employee records, customer information, contracts, or financial records could cause financial, legal, or reputational harm.

## Integrity

Impact Level: Moderate

Unauthorized modification of business records, contracts, system configurations, or financial information could disrupt business operations or cause incorrect decisions.

## Availability

Impact Level: Moderate

Loss of access to company systems, email, files, or remote access could significantly disrupt normal business operations.

## Overall System Categorization

Moderate
