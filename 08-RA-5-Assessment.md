# RA-5 - Vulnerability Monitoring and Scanning Assessment

## Control

RA-5 - Vulnerability Monitoring and Scanning

## Purpose

The purpose of this control is to ensure that the organization regularly identifies vulnerabilities in its systems and takes action to address them.

## Assessment Question

Does Sparkman Systems LLC regularly scan its systems for known vulnerabilities and track identified issues?

## Evidence Requested

To assess RA-5, the following evidence would be requested:

- Vulnerability scanning policy or procedure
- Recent vulnerability scan reports
- List of systems included in vulnerability scans
- Scan frequency or schedule
- Records showing how vulnerabilities are tracked
- Evidence of remediation or rescanning after fixes are applied

## Evidence Reviewed

The following fictional evidence was reviewed:

- Sparkman Systems LLC did not have a formal vulnerability scanning schedule
- No recent authenticated vulnerability scan reports were available
- The IT administrator occasionally used built-in security tools to identify issues
- Vulnerabilities were not tracked in a centralized register
- No documented process existed for verifying remediation through rescanning

## Assessment Result

Status: Not Satisfied

## Finding

Sparkman Systems LLC does not have a formal vulnerability scanning and remediation process. Regular authenticated scans are not performed, identified vulnerabilities are not centrally tracked, and remediation is not validated through rescanning.

## Risk

Undetected or unresolved vulnerabilities could remain present in the environment and be exploited by an attacker.

## Recommendation

Establish a recurring vulnerability scanning schedule, track findings in a centralized register, assign remediation owners and due dates, and perform rescans to verify that identified vulnerabilities have been resolved.
